Stanza is back.

If you read books on a phone around 2009, there is a fair chance you did it in Stanza. It went away for a long time. Stanza Redux is our attempt at bringing it back: a free EPUB reader with a library, a catalog browser, and not much else.

This is a placeholder post. We will replace it with a proper account of how the app came to be rebuilt, what changed in version 2, and what we plan to work on next.

Until then, the short version: open a catalog, pick a book, and read it.
